# App Store Connect > App Review Information > Notes (kopyala-yapıştır)

Turtle Dove is a private app for two people (couples / long-distance partners).
Most features open only after two accounts are paired with a 6-digit code, so a
single reviewer account cannot see them by itself. Nothing is hidden or
conditional on review: every feature is available to every paired user.

HOW TO SEE EVERYTHING WITHOUT A SECOND ACCOUNT
1. Launch the app. On the sign-in screen tap "Explore the app".
   (It is also on the pairing screen after sign-in.) No account is needed.
2. The in-app tour shows every feature with interactive sample data:
   pairing, voice/video calls, games (Trivia, Most Likely To, Word Game,
   Name-City-Animal, Turtle Deck), mood sharing, shared map, letters and voice
   messages, countdown, shared pet and conversation starters.
   The tour is available in all 5 app languages (TR, EN, DE, ES, FR).

Permissions: camera/microphone for calls, photo library for profile photo.
No features are enabled/disabled remotely or by region.

# Resolution Center cevabı (EN)

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for the feedback. We want to clarify that no feature in Turtle Dove
is hidden or concealed. Turtle Dove is a private app for two people, and its
shared features (calls, games, map, mood, letters, pet) become available only
after two accounts are paired. Our earlier review account was not paired, so
those screens were not reachable, which we understand looked like hidden
functionality. That was our oversight.

In this new build we added:
1. An account-free "Explore the app" tour (sign-in and pairing screens) that
   demonstrates every feature with sample data, in all supported languages.
2. Two demo accounts that are already paired (see App Review Information),
   so the full real experience can be tested.
3. Detailed Review Notes describing every feature.

No functionality is toggled remotely, by region, or by review status.
Please let us know if you need anything else.
